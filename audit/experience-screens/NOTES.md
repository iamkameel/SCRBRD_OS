# Experience screens: what each person sees

Evidence for a design pass on the whole product. Produced by `tools/shots-experience.mjs`; no app code changed.

- Widths: 390, 1366 (390 drawn as a touch phone, 1366 as a desktop). Themes: Daylight (colour scheme light), Floodlit (dark).
- Data: the seed plus tools/fixture-matchcentre.mjs's live fixture (Hilton College 1XI v Westville Boys' High 1XI). Invented names only (@example.invalid). People sign in by dev login, as the walks do. The seed holds no team manager, so the tool adds `manager@example.invalid` (teammanager, Hilton 1XI) to its own database; it holds no `sportsadmin`, so the school office (`registrar@`, schooladmin) stands for it.
- Pilot Matches: the tool turns the `pilot_sync` switch on and grants the seeded scorer in its own database, as smoke-browser-scorer-home does, so the scorer can start one.
- Audit: `narrowAudit` (tools/a11y-narrow.mjs), the narrow walk's own measurement, in the page: counts of sideways scroll, off-screen, clipped, overlap, text under 12px (small), taps under 44px (tiny), contrast below WCAG (weak). The 44px tap floor is a phone floor; read `tiny` at 1366 as secondary. Shell findings (sidebar, bottom bar) are counted in every row.
- Pictures: PNG, or JPEG at quality 70 where the PNG ran over about 300 KB; a page taller than 2400px is cut at 2400px. The shell scrolls its main, so the viewport is grown to the content's height first; on a phone the fixed bottom bar is drawn at the foot of the picture, over the last content.
- 246 pictures, 21 MB (PNGs over 100 KB re-saved as JPEG q70 afterwards).

## Destinations picked, and why

- **Director of sport** (sarah@example.invalid): matches, squad, analytics. Matches is the fixture list and the Match Centre, the thing a director opens first on match day; Squad is the people in his sides; Analytics is how the sides are playing. (Sarah is also a coach and a parent, so her menu is the staff menu with Family beside it.)
- **School office** (registrar@example.invalid): management, staff, squad. The office (schooladmin; the seed holds no sportsadmin) admits people and answers role requests: Management is where requests and role changes are decided, Staff is the register of who holds what, Squad is the pupils on the roll.
- **Coach** (coach@example.invalid): squad, matches, skills. A coach picks and reads his side (Squad), reads his fixtures (Matches) and records what a boy can do (Skills, the Develop group).
- **Team manager** (manager@example.invalid): readiness, logistics, squad. A team manager runs the day around a fixture: Readiness is who and what is ready, Logistics is transport and kit, Squad is who is turning up.
- **Scorer** (scorer@example.invalid): matches, readiness, calendar. Matches is where a scorer opens the pad; Readiness is the day's duties; Calendar is the fixtures coming up for him.
- **Parent** (parent@example.invalid): fixtures, notices, family. The parent's bar is Home (the child), Matches, Notices, Family; Home is the first shot and the other three are the destinations.
- **Pupil** (pillay@example.invalid): mymatches, passport, me. The pupil's bar is Home, Matches, Passport, Me; Home is the first shot and the other three are the destinations.
- **Spectator, signed in** (watcher@example.invalid): matches, calendar, competitions. A spectator account (watcher@, role spectator) is offered Matches, Calendar and Competitions from Play; those are what he can read.
- **Platform admin** (platform@example.invalid): management, modules, staff. The platform administrator's menu is short: Management (roles), Modules (features on and off, per school), Staff (people).
- **Spectator, signed out**: the public home page, the live match (Summary), its Scorecard and the pavilion display (`/display/:id`, the big screen). The signed-in spectator also has the Match Centre's Summary, Scorecard and Big screen.
- **Scorer, beyond the menu**: the pad on the live fixture, and a Pilot Match from the scorer's home up to the pad before the first ball (setup, schools, players, toss, openers, pad).
- **Parent, beyond the menu**: the child's terms (Home, the child, Read and agree). Linking a child is the family screen's links (shot) and, for an account with no school, the first-run request forms (`New account`).
- **New account**: the sign-in screen, the no-school state (a new Google account), and the onboarding flow (Log In, Create an account) step by step.
