# Scorer's Quick Guide

Scoring a match on the SCRBRD app. Print this page on A4 and fold to A5 (half size).

---

## Sign In

Open the app and tap **Get Started** or **Log In**. If the school offers it, tap your name; otherwise enter the login code from your school's office and tap **Sign In**.

---

## Open a Match

Tap **Match Centre** at the top left. Find the fixture (date, ground, teams) and tap **Open Live Scorer** or **Start Scoring**.

If this is a real match on your school's server, you will join it there. If it is a practice match on this phone only, the app will open the scoring pad.

---

## Set Up the Innings

If you see the toss question: tap whether the home or away side bats first. This is recorded and other scorers will see it.

If you see the openers or bowler sheets: pick the players from the list or type their names. Tap **Next** when you have answered.

---

## Score a Ball

The pad shows the score at the top and the over strip down the side.

1. Tap a delivery — a run (0 / 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 6), **Wide**, **No Ball**, or **Wicket**
2. If it is a run, the ball is scored at once
3. If it is a Wide or No Ball: you are asked runs off it; tap 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 or **Byes** (runs, not off the bat)
4. If it is a Wicket: name the batter out and how; fill in any fielder or runs; tap **Confirm Wicket**
5. The next batter sheet opens if an end is empty

---

## Undo a Ball

Tap the **⋯** menu at the top right. Tap **Undo**. The last ball is removed from the log and from the over strip. You can undo as far back as you want — there is no limit.

---

## No Signal — Keep Scoring

The app keeps a full scorecard on your phone. Every ball stays on the pad even if there is no signal. Tap the sync pill at the bottom to see:

- **Sent** — all balls are on the school's server
- **Sending 3** — 3 balls are on their way (the app sends them automatically)
- **Held 2** — the server refused 2 balls; they are kept on this phone until a person reviews them
- **On device** — no signal (or not signed in); balls are saved and will send when the signal comes back

Close the app, reload the page or walk away. When there is signal again, the app will send everything it has.

---

## Hand Over to Another Scorer

Only hand over at the end of an over, or if the phone is about to die.

**You (the outgoing scorer):**

1. Open the pad menu (**⋯**) and tap **Hand over**
2. Wait for the sync pill to say **Sent** — all your balls are on the server
3. If any balls show **Held** or are still **Sending**, stay where there is better signal
4. Tap **Arm handover**. A six-digit code appears
5. Read the code aloud to the next scorer

**The incoming scorer:**

1. Sign in on their phone
2. Open the same match
3. Open the pad menu (**⋯**) and tap **Hand over**, then tap **Take over**
4. Type the code and tap **Claim this match**
5. Check the board — runs, wickets, overs, batters — against the physical scoreboard
6. If the figures match, tap **Confirm**. The pad is now theirs
7. If they do not match, the app says what is wrong. Do not confirm; ask the outgoing scorer to check, and try again

---

## End of an Innings

When the last batter falls, the app will open the result sheet. Review it and tap **Accept** or **Amend**. The innings is then sealed and the next innings opens.

---

## Rain or Bad Light — Play Stopped

Open the pad menu (**⋯**) and tap **Play stopped**. Tell the app why (Rain, Bad light, Wet ground, or Other) and add a note if you want. Tap **Stop**. No balls can be scored while play is stopped.

When play resumes, open the menu (**⋯**) and tap **Resume play**. If the umpires cut the overs (for example, from 20 to 16), enter the new overs and the new target in the chase. Tap **Resume**.

---

## Phone Dies

If the phone dies or crashes during an innings:

1. Turn it back on
2. The app will reopen the match and the pad
3. Check the board against the physical scorebook — the sync pill will say what sent
4. Carry on scoring

If someone else picks up a phone from the ground with that match on it, they cannot score unless they sign in with their own account. They can see the score but cannot change it.
