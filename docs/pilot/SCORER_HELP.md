# Scorer's quick sheet

15 October 2026. Westville Boys' High School v [opponent].

## Sign in

Open `scrbrd-os.web.app/app`. Tap **Continue with Google**, or type your email and the office's code in **Sign-in code**, then **Sign In**. **Waiting for your school office** means the office has not linked you yet.

## Open the match

Tap **Match Centre** (bottom bar, or under **More**). On the fixture tap **Open Live Scorer →** if live, **Start Scoring →** if not. Answer the sheets: **The toss** (winner, then **Bat** or **Bowl**), openers, **Opening Bowler**. If a bar says **Can't score yet**, tap its button.

## Score a ball

The pad asks **Shot**, **Area**, **Outcome**: tap the runs on **Outcome**. For one tap a ball, switch on **Basic Scoring** in the **Pad menu** (three dots, right end of the title bar).

Under the keys: **Dot**, **Wide**, **No ball**, **Bye**, **Leg bye**. Tap an extra, then its runs.

**Wicket**: choose how out and the fielder, then **Confirm Out**. Pick the next batter on **Batting Order**, or type a name (J Smith) and tap **Go**. Pick each new bowler the same way.

**Undo**, beside **Dot**, takes back the last ball, even with no signal.

## The sync pill (under the scoreboard)

**Sent**: all balls are on the server. **Sending**: on their way. **Held**: no signal; saved on the phone, sent when it returns. **Refused**: the server turned balls down; tap the pill, read the reason, then **Record again** or **Discard**. **On device**: saved here only.

## No signal, or the phone dies

Keep scoring. Reopen the app; the pad returns with the score. If a bar says **Sign in**, tap it and sign in again.

## Hand over (both phones need signal)

Outgoing: wait for **Sent**. Tap **Hand over** in the title bar, then **Hand over scoring**. Read out the six-digit code. To stop: **Cancel — keep scoring myself**.

Incoming: sign in, open the same fixture, tap **Take over**, type the code, tap **Claim this match**. Read the physical scoreboard, not the app, and type its runs, wickets, overs and balls. Tap **Confirm and take over**. A wrong figure is refused. The old phone shows **Handed over**.

## End of an innings

The check sheet opens. Tap **That is right — close the innings**, or **Take back the last ball**. Then **Start 2nd Innings →**. After the second innings: **Match Complete**. Stay until the pill says **Sent**.

## Rain

**Pad menu**, **Play stopped**, a reason (**Rain**, **Bad light**, **Wet ground**, **Other**), **Stop play**. To restart tap **Resume**, type the umpires' overs (and the target in a chase), then the button at the bottom. If play cannot resume: **End innings (rain)**.
