# Coach's quick sheet

15 October 2026. Westville Boys' High School v [opponent].

## Sign in

Open `scrbrd-os.web.app/app`. Tap **Continue with Google**, or type your email and the office's code in **Sign-in code**, then **Sign In**. **Waiting for your school office** means the office has not linked you yet. A code works once: ask the office for a fresh one if it was used.

## Your match-day screen

On the **Dashboard**, a **Match day** card shows your next fixture: the opponent, the time, how many boys are named, the bus, and a count of signals. Tap **Open the Coach tab**, or **See the signals**.

The same screen is on the fixture: **Match Centre** (bottom bar, or under **More**), **Open match**, then **Coach**. No **Coach** tab means the office has not given you that side. Call [who to call].

Before the first ball it shows the day, **The side** and **Our bowlers this week**. While the match is on it shows the board and your bowlers with their overs left. Afterwards it shows each spell as the match log recorded it.

Tap **Signals** for the cards. Each says where it came from. Nothing on them is sent to anyone. **Seen** hides a card for you only.

## Availability

Tap **Squad**, then your side. The **Availability** panel lists each boy for the next fixture: **Available**, **Doubtful**, **Unavailable**, **Needs reconfirming**, or no answer. A boy or his family answers. You can answer for him: tap a word on his row. If the fixture changes, his answer is asked for again.

On a boy's card, **Set Availability** does the same for one boy. **The side** on the Coach tab shows the same words for the boys named.

## Follow a match live

Tap **Match Centre**, then **Open match** on the fixture. A live match refreshes by itself. Read **Summary**, **Scorecard** and **Commentary**.

## Practice match

Tap **Match Centre**, then **Open SCRBRD Scorer**, then **Start Practice Match**. Type the sides and names, and score a few balls. A practice match is kept on this phone only. It is never sent anywhere or counted. The scorer's own sheet is `docs/pilot/SCORER_HELP.md`.

## A boy's workload

Tap **Training**. **Bowling & training load** lists each boy's overs this week and this month, his longest spell, his sessions, and one word: spike, rising, steady, light or rested. It says "A guide to a conversation, not a diagnosis."

**Our bowlers this week** on the Coach tab shows the same, for your side.

You see only what your role may see. For an injured boy the app says he is restricted and when he is back. It never says why.

Trouble? Call [who to call].
