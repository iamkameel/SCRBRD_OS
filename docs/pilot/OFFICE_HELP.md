# School office quick sheet

15 October 2026. Westville Boys' High School v [opponent].

## The order of the load

The order is in `docs/pilot/PILOT_LOAD.md`, in "On 6 October: the order". Follow that table. This sheet only names the screens.

## Import players and guardians

Tap **Settings**, then **Import**. Under **What are you importing** choose **Players** or **Guardians**. Players go first, so that each guardian's child is on the roster.

Tap **Download the template**, fill it in, and save it as CSV. Under **CSV file** choose your file. Tap **Check**. It writes nothing and lists every line to fix. Fix the file and **Check** again. **Import** works only after a clean **Check** of that same file. Rows already at the school are updated, not duplicated.

## People

**Settings**, then **People**. Tap **+ Enrol a person**. Fill in **Full name**, **Email** and **Role**. A coach or team manager also needs **Which side**. A parent's or pupil's account needs **Which child**, chosen from the roster. Tick **Issue a sign-in code now** if the person signs in this week, then tap **Enrol**.

The window **Sign-in code** shows the code once. Write it down and hand it over in person or by phone, never by email. For someone already on the list, tap **Sign-in code** on their row for a new one.

At the top, **Google sign-ins waiting for you** lists people who signed in with Google. Read both addresses. If it is them, tap **Confirm it is them**. If you are not sure, tap **Issue a code instead**, or **Decline**.

## The Staff screen

Tap **Staff** (bottom bar, or under **More**). It lists everyone who holds a staff role. **Everyone** and the role buttons filter it. Tap a card for the email, the sides and the last sign-in. **Clearance register** shows each adult's checks, and how many are still to chase. Tap **Hide** or **Show**. You cannot change anything here: add people under **People**.

## A parent's answer, and the never-public mark

Tap **Squad**, your side, then the boy, then **Edit Profile**. Under **Public match pages** each guardian's answer is shown.

- A family says no, on the phone or in person: tap **Record a no, on the family's word**.
- A family says yes only on a signed form: tap **Record a yes from a signed form**. Fill in **Which form** and **The date it was signed**, then tap **Record the yes**.

For a boy who must never be named: under **Never show this child publicly** type the **Reason**, then tap **Never show this child publicly**. It beats every consent, on every public page. Only people who may set it can read the reason. To undo it, tap **Remove the mark**.

## A parent's link, and the family's agreement

Tap **Squad**, your side, the boy, then **Edit Profile**. Under **Guardian links**, choose the parent under **Parent or guardian**.

- A link not yet checked: tap **Verify the link**, then **Yes, verify**.
- The family signed the terms: tap **Record the agreement**, then **Yes, record it**. Verify first.

The page answers in words.

## Book a bus

Tap **Logistics**. Under **Book a bus**, tap **Add a vehicle**, then **Save the vehicle**. For a fixture your school hosts, tap **Book a trip**, fill it in, tap **Book this trip**, then **Yes, book it**. A booked trip cannot be changed here yet.

## Publish a fixture's public page

Tap **Match Centre**, then the fixture's card to open **Match Details**. Under **Public page** tap **Publish** beside your school's side. Only your own school's side shows the button. Players are named only where the family has said yes: everyone else is shown by position. The link is on the panel. To take it down, tap **Withdraw**.

If the panel says public pages are switched off, nothing is public yet. Call [who to call].
