# Parent's quick sheet

15 October 2026. Westville Boys' High School v [opponent].

## Sign in

Open `scrbrd.onrender.com/app`. Tap **Continue with Google**, or type your email and the school office's code in **Sign-in code**, then **Sign In**. A code works once. If it was used, ask the office for a new one.

## Link to your son

The school office links you to your son. You cannot do it yourself.

If you used Google, the screen may say **Waiting for your school office**. The office is checking that the Google account is yours. When they have, tap **Continue with Google** again. Or ask them for a sign-in code.

Until you are linked, **Family** says "No child is linked to your account yet." Ask the office.

If you see **Join a school**, choose **I am a parent or guardian**, and say which child. The school answers. Nothing is given to you until they do.

## Follow his match live

Open **Home**. While your son's side is playing, a **Live** card shows the team's score, and his own line when he is batting. Tap **Follow the match**.

You can also tap **Matches**, then the match under **Live now**. Past matches are under **Played**.

## Show your son on public match pages

Tap **Family**, your son's card, then **Consents**. The first switch reads **Show [name] on public match pages**. It is **Off** until you turn it **On**.

What it means:

- If you say yes, public match pages show your son by his initial and surname. They never show a full name, a first name alone, a photo or a date of birth.
- Until you say yes, he is shown by position: "Batter" or "Bowler".
- Saying no takes effect at once, on every public page, finished scorecards too. You can change your answer at any time.
- From eighteen, the decision is his own.

The school can also keep a boy off public pages, whatever any family says.

## If you are worried about a child

The hand icon at the top of the screen is **Raise a concern**. It opens **Safeguarding**. **Your DSO** there names your school's Designated Safeguarding Officer. The app shows a name, not a phone number. Tap **Raise a concern** on that screen to tell the DSO. You do not need to be sure.

If a child is in danger now, phone SAPS on 10111 or Childline on 116.

Trouble? Call [who to call].
