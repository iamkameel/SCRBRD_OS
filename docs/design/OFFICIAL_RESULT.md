## 0 · In one page

Today a finished match has a status (`complete`) and a result read off the log, and nothing that says a second person looked at that result and stood behind it. "Looks right" dismisses a banner on one phone. An organiser's decision row is a different thing (a walkover is not a confirmation). So the honesty-labels build was right: nothing can be called official.

The design adds **one stored act, pinned to the log's revision**: a *confirmation* row naming the match, the head `seq` and the `result_hash` it was given for, who gave it and when. "Official" is then **derived**, exactly as the result is: a result is official when a standing confirmation names the current head and the current hash. A correction of any kind moves the head (or, for a decision, the hash), so the confirmation stops matching by itself: the result is provisional again, visibly, without anyone remembering to flip a flag; the row stays as the record of what was once official, and a trigger stamps when and why it lapsed and tells both schools. Re-confirmation is a new row. The confirmer is never a hand that wrote the log: not the scorer of any ball, not the requester or the approver of an amendment folded into this head, not the releaser of a held ball. For a competition fixture the organiser confirms; for a friendly the home school's director of sport or principal does, and the away school has a window in which to dispute.

Three words, and only two of them public: **Provisional** (complete, not confirmed, or confirmed and since corrected), **Under review** (staff only: a dispute stands), **Official result**. Standings, progression, careers and honours keep reading the log as they do now; the table says how many of its results are provisional, and nothing downstream waits for the word.

## 1 · Decisions

| # | decision | recommendation | alternatives, and why not |
|---|---|---|---|
| **D1** | What "official" is | **Derived, from a stored confirmation pinned to a revision.** `match_result_confirmation(match_id, head_seq, result_hash, confirmed_by, confirmed_at, …)`; `match_official(m)` says `official` iff a standing row has `head_seq = max(seq)` of the match's log **and** `result_hash = match_result(m).result_hash`. Two pins: the head catches every correction to the log (I36 D1); the hash catches a decision recorded or withdrawn and a status change, which move the outcome without an event | a fifth `match.status` value `official` (no: `db/00` is frozen, and a status is a flag that must be flipped back by every correction path — a silent un-confirmation waiting to happen); a `confirmed boolean` on `match` (the same); hash only (no: a corrected 4 that leaves the outcome unchanged still changes a boy's figures, and the scorecard, not only the winner, is what was confirmed) |
| **D2** | Who confirms a competition fixture | **The organiser**: `result.confirm` held at the competition by `competitionadmin` (and superadmin). The table and the bracket are his; one school confirming a league result is one school speaking for two | either school's director of sport, with the organiser overriding (no: the home side signs off its own win); both schools (no: the away side is often not on the platform, PUBLIC_DATA L5, and two signatures stall every weekend) |
| **D3** | Who confirms a friendly | **The home school's `result.confirm` holder** (director of sport, principal; the row check of D5 applies). The away school gets the dispute window of D7, not a signature | the scorer (no: one pair of hands, and `scoring.finalise` is not even checked by a route); nobody — a friendly stays provisional for ever (no: a friendly feeds careers and honours too) |
| **D4** | How a competition says who confirms | **A key in the competition's conditions, read through the match's own frozen document** as the table keys are (db/61, db/69): `results.confirmed_by ∈ {organiser, home_school, either_school}`, default `organiser`; `results.dispute_days`, default 7. A friendly has no competition and takes `home_school` | a column on `competition` (no: SCRBRD-114 D1 is one model per competition, frozen per match; a competition whose rule changes mid-season must not reopen played matches) |
| **D5** | Separation of duties | **The confirmer is not a hand on this head**: not `scorer_user_id` of any event in the log at this head; not `requested_by` of any applied amendment nor `approved_by` in any void's payload at this head; not `resolved_by` of any released held ball; not the confirmer of a scorebook import of this match. A row check in `match_result_confirm()`, refusal `you_wrote_this_log`; asserted in `separation.test.mjs` that `result.confirm` and `scoring.amend.request` are held by disjoint role sets (as the two amendment capabilities are) | a role-set rule alone (no: the director of sport holds `scoring.edit` and may have scored; the row check is what keeps his own match out of his hands) |
| **D6** | When a result may be confirmed | **`match_confirmable(m)`**: `status ∈ (complete, abandoned)`; every innings in the log sealed and the seal standing (R3's `match_verified`), or a standing decision row, or `abandoned` with no innings; no `scoring_amendment` pending; no `ball_event_quarantine` row unresolved; no standing confirmation at this head. No earliest time, no waiting period: a Saturday result can be official at tea. The organiser's queue (GA-I09 O-row) nudges at 48 h unconfirmed | automatic "official after N days" (no: it labels official what nobody looked at — the honesty rule the labels build exists for) |
| **D7** | Disputes and the window | **A dispute is a stamp on the standing confirmation** (`disputed_by/at`, a note of 10+ characters) by a `result.confirm` holder of **either** side or the organiser, within `results.dispute_days` of `confirmed_at`. It moves the staff label to "Under review"; it changes no figure, no standing, no progression. It ends when (a) a correction lapses the confirmation (the ordinary path: the disputant's school files an amendment, the second signature decides it), or (b) the organiser — for a friendly, a `result.confirm` holder who is not the disputant — clears it with a note, and the result stands official. After the window: no dispute, only an amendment, which already needs two hands | a dispute object of its own with its own screen (no: it would be a fourth queue; the amendment is the dispute with a key); a dispute that un-confirms at once (no: a note from one side would knock a result off the table) |
| **D8** | Does it ever lock? | **No.** An amendment may be approved at any time; a wrong figure about a child must stay correctable (POPIA accuracy). The control is that an approval on an official result **reopens it visibly and tells both schools and the organiser** (D9), and the approver's sheet says so before he taps. Confirmation is re-done, by the same rule, by a hand that was not on the correction | lock after the window, corrections by the organiser only (no: a locked log needs a door, and every door is a capability a child's correction then waits on) |
| **D9** | A correction after confirmation | **Lapse, say so, re-confirm.** Any `ball_event` appended (a void, a released ball, a late ball, an import), a decision recorded or withdrawn, or a status change on a match with a standing confirmation: the row gets `lapsed_at`, `lapsed_seq` and `lapsed_reason ∈ (correction, decision, status)` from a trigger; `match_official()` already says `provisional` without the stamp (D1) — the stamp is the audit and the hook, never the truth. The "official" notice is retracted `superseded` (NOTIFICATIONS D18) and one medium notice says the result was reopened; a new confirmation writes a new row and a new notice | delete the row (no: the audit of what was once official is gone); keep it official if `result_hash` is unchanged (no: D1's second alternative) |
| **D10** | What waits for "official" | **Nothing that exists today.** Standings (`db/69`) keep counting every played match; progression (`db/72`) keeps resolving and flagging on the hash as built; careers, milestones, records and honours keep reading the log. The table gains `provisional_results` beside `unattested_results`, and the public standing says "includes 2 provisional results". Recognition's `match_verified` stays the scorer's seal tier (R3) and this design does not move D6, D16 or D25 — but see Q4 | hold a provisional result out of the table (no: a table with holes misleads more than a count); gate the next knockout round on official (no: a cup draw is made the same evening; db/72's flag already covers a reopen) |
| **D11** | The words | **Three states, two of them public**: *Provisional*, *Under review* (staff only), *Official result*. The public and the parent see time only, team-level, never who confirmed or disputed, never a reason — "Official result · confirmed 20:15, 11 Oct"; "Provisional"; a reopened result reads "Provisional · corrected 18:42" with I36's chip. Staff see role words: "confirmed by the organiser"; "disputed by Kearsney, 12 Oct · under review". "Under review" never reaches a public page: it names a dispute between schools about a match that may be about a boy's innings (I36 D12's rule, extended) | three public words (no: D12); "Unofficial" (no: it reads as "doubtful"; "provisional" reads as "not yet") |
| **D12** | The component | **`StateLabel` gains three kinds** (`provisional`, `review`, `official`) beside `demo`, `practice`, `readonly`: text and an icon, never colour alone, 12 px floor, not tappable; `why` carries the time or the role words. I36's "Corrected 18:42" chip (44 px, opens the correction line) stays the tappable thing beside it. The header comment that says "there is no official" is rewritten to say where the state comes from | a badge of its own per screen (no: GA-I21 made one label so nobody takes a demo for a record; this is the same honesty) |
| **D13** | The scorer's and coach's "Looks right" | **Stays a device-only dismiss, reworded**: "Checked on this phone · this does not make the result official; the organiser does that" (role word by fixture class). A `result.confirm` holder who passes D5 sees "Confirm result" in its place, which writes. Nobody else sees a confirm | remove the prompt (no: it is the scorer's own check and the door to the amendment flow) |
| **D14** | Withdrawing a confirmation | **The confirmer may withdraw his own within the dispute window, with a note** (`withdrawn_by/at/note`; a tap on the wrong fixture happens). After the window, only a correction reopens it. The withdrawal is said to both sides like a lapse | never (no: a mis-tap then needs a fake amendment); anyone with the capability (no: a second person un-confirming is a dispute, D7) |

## 2 · States, and how each is known

| state | `match_official(m).state` | known by | public word | staff word |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| scheduled, live | `not_finished` | `match.status` | the live labels as today | — |
| complete, not confirmable | `provisional` with `blocked_by` | D6's checks: `innings_unsealed`, `amendment_pending`, `ball_held` | "Provisional" | "Provisional · 1 correction awaiting approval" (where I36's policy admits the reader) |
| complete, confirmable, no row | `provisional` | no standing row | "Provisional" | "Provisional · awaiting confirmation by the organiser" |
| confirmed at this head and hash | `official` | standing row matches | "Official result · confirmed 20:15, 11 Oct" | "Official result · confirmed by the organiser, 20:15" |
| confirmed, disputed, window open | `under_review` | `disputed_at` set, not cleared | **"Provisional"** (D11) | "Under review · disputed by Kearsney, 12 Oct" |
| confirmed, then the head or hash moved | `provisional`, `lapsed_at` set | row no longer matches; stamp from D9's trigger | "Provisional · corrected 18:42" | "Provisional · was official 20:15, 11 Oct · reopened by a correction 18:42, 13 Oct · needs confirming again" |
| re-confirmed | `official` (a new row) | the new row matches | as official | as official; the audit shows both rows |
| not reachable | — | the read failed | I08's words: "Could not refresh · showing the match as at …" | the same |

The time is `confirmed_at` (or `lapsed_at`) in the fixture's time zone; the date once it is not today. Nothing moves under a finger: a state change learned on a poll shows I36's "Updated · refresh" line first.

## 6 · Tests

**`db/99` §69 (SQL, seeded fixtures).** The scorer of the seeded complete match confirms → `you_wrote_this_log`. The director confirms while an amendment is pending → `amendment_pending`; while a held ball stands → `ball_held`; on a `live` match → `not_finished`; on a complete match with an unsealed innings → `innings_unsealed`. The requester, then the approver, of an applied amendment each confirm → `you_wrote_this_log`. A clean confirmer confirms → one row, `head_seq = max(seq)`, `result_hash` equal to `match_result()`'s, `match_official().state = 'official'`, one `fixture` notice per side, low; a second confirm → `already_official`. Then a void is appended → the row stamped `correction`, state `provisional`, the official notice `retracted_at` set with `superseded`, one medium "reopened" notice per side; re-confirm → a second row, the first kept. A decision withdrawn → `lapsed_reason = 'decision'`; status complete → abandoned → `status`. A dispute by the away director → `under_review`; by the scorer → `not_permitted`; after `results.dispute_days` → `window_closed`; cleared by the disputant → `cannot_clear_your_own`; cleared by the organiser → `official`. The public read answers `official`/`provisional` and never `under_review`; `public_competition_standing().provisional_results` counts the seeded league's unconfirmed matches; `competition_standing` points are identical before and after confirmation (D10). A withdrawal after the window → `window_closed`. A friendly's confirmer is the home school's; an away director → `not_permitted`. The pad's credential cannot call any door.

**Unit (`packages/policy/test`).** `separation.test.mjs`: the two disjoint sets of §5. `notifications.mjs`: the `fixture` kind's words.

**Walks.** `tools/smoke-official.mjs` (API, extending `smoke-amend.mjs`'s seeded match): confirm, reopen by amendment, re-confirm, dispute and clear, each refusal above in words; the public page read in the same process shows the new label at once (N2's drop), and a second process within the TTL. `smoke-browser-matchcentre.mjs`: the label on the result header; "Confirm result" absent for the scorer, present for the director, the sheet's words; the Corrections sheet's reopening warning on an official result. `smoke-browser-public.mjs`: "Official result" and "Provisional" rendered; the rendered page contains no "review", no name, no note. `smoke-a11y`: the label at 12 px+, the sheet's buttons 44 px, the state change announced in a live region. `smoke-fold-figures.mjs`: standings parity unchanged by a confirmation.
